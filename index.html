FAQ

Quick answers

Is this only for events?

No. The concept covers cultural, educational, corporate, community, public, sports, and professional events, subject to configured modules.

Can it connect to databases and automate tasks?

Yes, integrations and automation can be planned. Exact databases, APIs, workflows, access controls, and recurring costs are determined during discovery and quoted as part of the scope.

Can every organization have a separate website?

Yes. The shared framework can be configured with organization-specific content, branding, event settings, and a separate registration destination.

Will registration data be shared between organizations?

The intended setup keeps each organization’s registrations in its own spreadsheet. Access is granted only to authorized people as agreed.

Can we use our own domain?

Yes. You can purchase the domain in your own name and request connection assistance. Domain and related service costs may be additional.

Are registration forms customized for each event?

Yes. Registration forms are our own website-integrated forms, customized according to the event requirements. The form fields, submission workflow, and data destination are agreed during setup and depend on the selected package.

Does every package include online payment?

No. Payment gateway integration is not implied by the listed starting packages. It must be requested, scoped, and quoted separately.

Are the listed prices final?

They are starting prices, not guaranteed final quotes. Final pricing depends on requirements and is confirmed before payment.

How long does an event website take to launch?

Delivery time depends on the agreed pages, form fields, integrations, content readiness, and review cycle. The target timeline will be confirmed in the project proposal before work begins.

Who owns the event content and registration data?

Your organization retains ownership of the content and event data it provides. Data access, export format, storage destination, and handover arrangements will be agreed before setup.

Does the package include ongoing maintenance and support?

Ongoing maintenance, updates, support duration, and response expectations are not automatically included unless listed in the confirmed project scope.

What happens if we need extra features?

Additional pages, fields, integrations, automation, or changes beyond the agreed scope can be reviewed and quoted separately before implementation.
